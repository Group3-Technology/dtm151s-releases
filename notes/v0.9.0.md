## DTM 151 Teslameter Control 0.9.0

Desktop control and monitoring for the **Group3 DTM 151-S digital
teslameter** over a serial connection — live field and temperature readout,
real-time plotting, CSV logging, and a configurable derived measure.

This release makes units dependable end to end: a log is always in one unit,
the readout shows the instrument's full resolution, and your Tesla/Gauss
choice is remembered. It also adds hand-set graph ranges and export, and
fixes a number of reliability issues. **Recommended for everyone.**

**New here?** Download the file for your platform below, then follow the
[install steps](https://github.com/Group3-Technology/dtm151s-releases#installing)
— there's a one-time approval on first launch, because the application isn't
code-signed. No installer and no admin rights are needed. The full **User
Guide** is included in the download, and `SHA256SUMS.txt` is attached if
you'd like to verify what you downloaded.

## What's new

### Your Tesla/Gauss choice is remembered

The Tesla/Gauss button is remembered between sessions. When the application
connects — and after a RESET, a power cycle or a reconnect — it sets the
instrument to that unit. Until you have chosen one, the instrument uses its
own DIP-switch unit, as before.

### Set the graph range by hand, and export it

Right-click any graph pane for:

- **View All** — fit that pane to its data.
- **Y axis → Manual** — type a minimum and maximum, in the unit the axis
  shows (on an axis in mT, type 1 and 9 for 1 mT to 9 mT). The graph keeps
  scrolling while your range holds.
- **Export…** — save the graph as an image (PNG), SVG or CSV.

The **Autorange** button goes out while any pane has a range set by hand;
press it to fit every pane again. A range you set stays on the same data if
you switch Tesla/Gauss or °C/°F.

## Fixed

- **A log could switch unit part-way through.** Pressing the Tesla/Gauss
  button during a log changed the instrument's own unit, so some readings
  were logged in the other unit. The button now changes only what you see
  on screen, and every log is written in one unit (the one on screen when
  logging started), stated in its `unit` column.
- **The `unit` column could read `?` on every row** when the instrument
  wasn't sending units with its readings. The application now sets the
  instrument's unit when it connects and labels every reading with it.
- **MIN and MAX could show the wrong unit or magnitude** — for example a
  minimum of 0.4 mT shown as "400 T", or an extreme recorded before a unit
  change shown in the new unit. Each now keeps the unit it was measured in.
- **Starting a new capture overwrote the previous log** when the log path
  was the same. A new capture now goes beside it as `name-2.csv`,
  `name-3.csv`, and so on. Choosing an existing file with **Browse…** and
  confirming **Replace** still overwrites it, once.
- **Run Script could stay greyed out** for the rest of the session after
  the link dropped mid-script.
- **The derived-measure graph lost its history** while its tab wasn't on
  screen.
- **A presets file saved in a non-UTF-8 editor stopped the application
  starting.** It now falls back to the built-in presets.
- Smoother reconnecting, and several smaller reliability fixes.

## Changed

- **Readings show in plain Gauss or Tesla at full resolution** — gauss to 3
  decimals (`+10251.210 G`), tesla to 7 (`+1.0251210 T`) — instead of
  switching between µT, mT, T or mG, G, kG with three decimals. MIN and MAX
  follow the same format.
- **CSV field values are written to 9 decimals** in the log's unit.
- **The derived-measure unit is limited to 10 characters**, and a long
  derived label is shortened on screen (the full label is in its tooltip)
  instead of widening the display.

## Upgrading from 0.8.0

Unzip and run as usual.

**Your saved settings, CSV log files and derived-measure presets are kept.**
Nothing has moved, and no CSV column has been added or renamed. Two small
things to know:

- The first time you choose Tesla or Gauss, that choice is saved. From then
  on the application sets the instrument to it at every connect, so the
  instrument's front panel follows the application rather than its DIP
  switch.
- A derived-measure unit longer than 10 characters is shortened to 10 when
  it loads. Because the CSV's derived column is named after its unit, that
  column's name changes to match.
