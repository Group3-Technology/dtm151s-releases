## DTM 151 Teslameter Control 0.7.3

A housekeeping release. There are no new features, and nothing changes about
how the instrument is controlled, displayed, or logged — if 0.7.2 is working
for you, upgrading is optional.

One thing does change on your machine, and it is handled for you.

### Changed

**Your saved settings have moved to a new location.**

The application now stores its preferences under a Group3 Technology folder
rather than the previous one. This covers the remembered serial port and baud
rate, the acquisition mode, the Block sample count and rate, the CSV logging
path, the CONSOLE save directory, and the derived-measure configuration.

**No action is needed.** The first time you launch 0.7.3 it imports your
existing settings automatically, so it should come up exactly as you left it.
Your old settings are left in place and untouched — delete them whenever you
like, or leave them alone.

The new locations, for reference:

- **Windows** — `HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\Group3 Technology\DTM 151 TESLAMETER`
- **macOS** — `~/Library/Preferences/com.group3technology.DTM 151 TESLAMETER.plist`

The application's log file has moved to a matching folder. Old logs are not
carried over — they are diagnostic only, and safe to delete.

### Upgrading

Unzip and run as usual — see the README for the first-launch step on each
platform. Your settings carry across automatically.

If the application does start with default settings, your previous preferences
have not been lost; they are still in the old location. Please get in touch and
let us know which operating system you are on.
