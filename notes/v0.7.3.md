## DTM 151 Teslameter Control 0.7.3

Desktop control and monitoring for the **Group3 DTM 151-S digital
teslameter** over a serial connection — live field and temperature readout,
real-time plotting, CSV logging, and a configurable derived measure.

**First time here?** Download the file for your platform below, then follow
the [install steps](https://github.com/Group3-Technology/dtm151s-releases#installing)
— there's a one-time click to approve the app on first launch, because it
isn't code-signed. No installer and no admin rights are needed. The full
**User Guide** is included in the download, and `SHA256SUMS.txt` is attached
if you'd like to verify what you downloaded.

## If you're upgrading from 0.7.2

This is a housekeeping release. There are no new features, and nothing
changes about how the instrument is controlled, displayed, or logged.

One thing does change on your machine, and it is handled for you.

### Your saved settings have moved

The application now stores its preferences under a Group3 Technology folder
rather than the previous one. This covers the remembered serial port and
baud rate, the acquisition mode, the Block sample count and rate, the CSV
logging path, the CONSOLE save directory, and the derived-measure
configuration.

**No action is needed.** The first time you launch 0.7.3 it imports your
existing settings automatically, so it should come up exactly as you left
it. Your old settings are left in place and untouched — delete them whenever
you like, or leave them alone.

The new locations, for reference:

- **Windows** — `HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\Group3 Technology\DTM 151 TESLAMETER`
- **macOS** — `~/Library/Preferences/com.group3technology.DTM 151 TESLAMETER.plist`

The application's log file has moved to a matching folder. Old logs are not
carried over — they are diagnostic only, and safe to delete.

### Upgrading

Unzip and run as usual. Your settings carry across on first launch.

If the application does start with default settings, your previous
preferences have not been lost — they are still in the old location above.
Please get in touch and let us know which operating system you are on.
